autopost

autopost is a personal tool used by one creator to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok channels at scheduled times. It runs on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to other users.

What it does: it uploads a video file the creator has prepared and approved, sets its title, description, thumbnail and publish time, and adds a reminder to the creator's own Google Calendar when a post succeeds or fails.
